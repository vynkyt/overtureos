# Guide to OvertureOS

## To open windows:
Click on the icon of desired application to open its window!

## To close windows:
Click on the icons of desired application again to close its window. Alternatively, click on the "x" button on the top right corner of the window.

## Window functionality:
- **Resizable**: hover mouse on the sides of a window, then click and hold to resize.
- **Draggable**: click and hold on window headings to drag windows around :)
- **Fullscreen**: click on the first bright pink dot on the top left corner of the window to enter / exit fullscreen mode!
